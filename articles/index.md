# Articles

### Tutorials

Each tutorial answers one question with real or simulated data, from
start to finish.

- [1. Mapping crt K76T in the Greater Horn of
  Africa](https://mrc-ide.github.io/stareal/articles/crt76t.md):

  From a STAVE data object to monthly maps, country trends and
  probability statements: the chloroquine-resistance marker crt K76T
  across the eight IGAD countries, 1989-2024.

- [2. Rare and emerging markers: k13 R561H in East
  Africa](https://mrc-ide.github.io/stareal/articles/k13-561h.md):

  Mapping a marker that most surveys never find: the artemisinin
  partial-resistance mutation k13 R561H, from Rwanda into north-western
  Tanzania and south-western Uganda.

- [3.
  Covariates](https://mrc-ide.github.io/stareal/articles/covariates.md):

  Covariates by area, by time step, and by both: how to build the
  tables, how to read the coefficients, and what happens when the data
  cannot inform one.

- [4. Cross-validation and model
  comparison](https://mrc-ide.github.io/stareal/articles/crossval.md):

  Which model predicts held-out surveys best, and are its intervals
  calibrated? Random and spatial cross-validation, and comparing models
  on the same folds.

- [5. Simulate, fit,
  check](https://mrc-ide.github.io/stareal/articles/simulation.md):

  Data simulated from known parameters at the survey design of tutorial
  1: does the fit recover the truth, and are its intervals honest?

### Method

- [Method](https://mrc-ide.github.io/stareal/articles/method.md):

  The model, why it can be fitted on the observed cells only, the
  sampler, and how predictions and group summaries are made.
